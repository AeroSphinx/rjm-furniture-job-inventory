## Business rules
- Confirming a transaction inserts the ledger row AND updates materials.current_stock in ONE DB transaction (Postgres function exposed as RPC). Client must never update current_stock directly - revoke that ability and explain how.
- Stock never below zero. Out-of-stock materials cannot be issued to a job (enforce in the function; UI shows disabled option with explanation).
- Stock status: Out of Stock when current_stock = 0; Low Stock when current_stock <= minimum_level (and > 0); otherwise In Stock.
- Job material cost = sum(quantity x unit_cost_at_time) over that job's stock_out transactions.
- Deletes are soft (deleted_at) with Undo. Workers cannot edit completed jobs.

# ADDENDUM 2 — SCOPE REMINDER FROM THE USER (authoritative; applies to every remaining step: design, design-review, plan, verify)

Those are correctness requirements, not "production polish," and must not be weakened. The distinction being drawn here is: build these because they are *right*, not because they chase production-grade completeness (e.g. no need for audit logging, rate limiting, retry queues, observability/metrics, multi-region, or anything a real SaaS would need but a 4-day graded prototype does not).
